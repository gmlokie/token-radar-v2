How the value is fitted — not asserted

A single multiple overstates a large protocol tenfold: the comparables' market-cap/TVL falls from 0.42 at $5–50M of TVL to 0.02 at $1–5B. So the value is a curve fitted on the 92 tokens that already trade, winsorised at the 90th percentile, and the curve is tested out of sample before it prices anything. A category multiple was tried and rejected by the same test: it worsened the leave-one-out error from ×2.89 to ×3.59. The board's 198 rows come from an audit funnel over the snapshot's 758 protocols: 342 are strict pre-token, and 144 of those are excluded as wrapped, staked, bridged, exchange or versioned products of a token that already trades (CEX 42, liquid staking 38, bridges 43, versioned names 11, wrapped or staked names 5, restaking 5).

The size curve

Each dot is one of the 92 tokens that already trade (log TVL against log market cap). The line is the fitted curve. Big protocols sit low on the line: that is why a large protocol's implied value is smaller than its TVL times a flat multiple. The shaded band is the curve's typical-error band — the line ± the leave-one-out median error (×2.89) — so about half the comparables land inside it, and a dot outside it is a protocol the curve cannot price. The 95% band is ±×28: too wide to draw, and that is itself the point — the curve locates the order of magnitude, not the price.

Research note — the method, the tests, the limits

The full note is research.md: the value is a curve fitted on the 92 tokens that already trade (log10(market cap) = 3.5118 + 0.4458 × log10(TVL)), tested out of sample — leave-one-out median error ×2.89, blind time-split ×2.14, blind random 80/20 split ×2.02, bootstrap slope 0.26–0.63 — with R² 0.16 stated honestly. A category multiple was tried and rejected (×2.89 → ×3.59), and so was a multiple regression on growth, age and chains (time-split ×2.14 → ×15.84). The full pipeline — curve × multipliers — is tested too: leave-one-out median error ×2.80, and leave-one-covariate-out: dropping maturity worsens it to ×3.14, growth to ×2.98, the winsor cap to ×3.28, and chains barely moves it (×2.73 — neutral, chains stays, with the evidence stated in the research note). The chart's shaded band is the curve's typical-error band; the 95% band is ±×28. The board's 198 rows are the complete rankable set under the strict pre-token definition.
